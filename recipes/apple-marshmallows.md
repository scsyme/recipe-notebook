# Apple marshmallows

One plateful.

## Ingredients

- 4 small apples (about 300–400 g)
- About 100 ml water
- 30–40 g powdered gelatine
- Icing sugar or cornflour for dusting, optional

## Method

1. Simmer the apples in the water until completely soft, then blend to a smooth purée.
2. Stir the gelatine into the warm purée until it dissolves.
3. Whisk on high for 15–30 minutes. Look for a pale, fluffy mixture roughly three times its original volume.
4. Transfer to a lined dish and refrigerate for 2–4 hours until set. Cut into squares and dust lightly if sticky.
