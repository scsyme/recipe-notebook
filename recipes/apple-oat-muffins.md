# Apple oat muffins

Makes 12 muffins.

## Ingredients

- 2 apples (about 200 g), grated
- 2 large eggs
- 160 g rolled oats (2 cups in the source)
- A squeeze of honey (roughly 30 g or less)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to about 175°C (350°F). The oven setting is unspecified.
2. Stir the grated apple, eggs, oats and honey together.
3. Divide the mixture between the holes of a 12-hole muffin tin.
4. Bake for about 30 minutes, until set and golden. Leave to cool.
