# Aquafaba chocolate mousse

Serves 2.

## Ingredients

- About 120 ml aquafaba (the liquid drained from a tin of chickpeas)
- 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (about 5 g)
- A splash of sugar-free maple syrup, to taste (roughly 30 ml in the earlier extraction)

## Method

1. Beat the aquafaba until it holds stiff peaks.
2. Gently fold in the cocoa and syrup, keeping as much air in the mixture as possible.
3. Spoon into two dishes and refrigerate. The source gives no chilling time.
