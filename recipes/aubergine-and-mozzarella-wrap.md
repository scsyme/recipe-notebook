# Aubergine and mozzarella wrap

Serves 1.

## Ingredients

- ½ large aubergine (about 200 g)
- Salt
- 15 g light mozzarella, grated
- 20 g low-fat mozzarella, sliced
- 50 g tomatoes, sliced
- 20 g rocket
- 1 tsp balsamic glaze (about 5 ml)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the aubergine into thin rounds, sprinkle with salt, leave for 5 minutes and pat dry.
2. Scatter half the grated mozzarella over a lined baking tray. Overlap the aubergine rounds in a rectangle and scatter the remaining grated cheese over them.
3. Bake for 27–30 minutes until golden with crisp edges. Leave for 3–4 minutes before lifting.
4. Add the tomatoes, rocket, sliced mozzarella and balsamic glaze. Roll tightly and cut in half.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
