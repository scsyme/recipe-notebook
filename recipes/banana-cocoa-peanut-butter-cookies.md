# Banana, cocoa and peanut butter cookies

Makes about 12 cookies. This recipe uses **peanut butter powder**.

## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe, spotty bananas
- 25 g cocoa powder
- 50 g peanut butter powder
- Chocolate chips, optional (quantity unspecified)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a baking tray. The oven setting is unspecified.
2. Mash the bananas. Stir in the cocoa and peanut butter powder, then fold in the chocolate chips if using.
3. Spoon about 12 portions onto the lined tray.
4. Bake for 15 minutes.
