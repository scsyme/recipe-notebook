# Carrot cake with cream cheese icing

Makes 9 large squares.

## Ingredients

- 300 g low-fat cottage cheese
- 2 eggs and 150 g egg whites
- 100 g oat flour or finely blended oats
- 2 tsp each baking powder and vanilla extract
- 2½–3 tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- ¼ tsp each ground nutmeg and salt
- Sweetener to taste
- 200 g finely grated carrot
- Oil spray for the dish
- **For the icing:** 150 g light cream cheese, 200 g 0% Greek yoghurt, 1 tsp vanilla extract, sweetener to taste and a pinch of salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175°C. Grease a 23 × 23 cm baking dish with oil spray.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, egg whites, oats, baking powder, vanilla, spices, salt and sweetener until smooth. Fold in the carrot and pour into the dish.
3. Bake for 28–35 minutes, checking from 25 minutes. The centre should be set and a skewer should come out with moist crumbs. Leave to cool.
4. Suggested finish: stir the icing ingredients together, spread over the cooled cake and cut into nine squares.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
