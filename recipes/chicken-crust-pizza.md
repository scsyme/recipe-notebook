# Chicken-crust pizza

Makes 1 pizza.

## Ingredients

- 450 g chicken mince
- 2 eggs
- About 50 g Parmesan, grated
- 1 tsp each garlic powder and Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp each salt and black pepper
- About 120 ml pizza sauce
- About 100 g mozzarella
- Pepperoni, optional

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the chicken, eggs, Parmesan and seasonings.
2. Spread the mixture thinly and evenly on a baking tray to form the base. Bake for 25 minutes.
3. Let the base rest for 5 minutes. Raise the oven temperature to 220°C.
4. Spread with sauce, add the mozzarella and any pepperoni, and bake for another 10 minutes until bubbling. Let it stand briefly before slicing.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.

## Food safety

Check the thickest part of the chicken base with a clean food thermometer before serving. The UK Food Standards Agency gives 75°C for 30 seconds as one safe combination. [Cooking your food](https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/cooking-your-food)
