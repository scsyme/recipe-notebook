# Cottage cheese crisps

Makes about 9 large crisps.

## Ingredients

- 300 g fat-free cottage cheese
- Herbs or spices, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. The oven setting is unspecified.
2. Divide the cottage cheese into about nine portions on a baking tray. Spread each thinly and season to taste.
3. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until golden.
4. Leave to cool for 10 minutes before lifting the crisps from the tray.
