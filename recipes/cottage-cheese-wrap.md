# Cottage cheese wrap

Serves 1.

## Ingredients

- 225 g fat-free cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- Seasoning to taste, such as garlic powder, salt and pepper
- 2 bacon medallions
- About 5 g Parmesan, grated
- A handful of spinach
- A few cherry tomatoes, sliced

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a tray with baking parchment.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs and seasoning until smooth. Spread thinly over the parchment.
3. Bake for about 30 minutes until golden. The caption warns that a pale wrap can stay mushy.
4. Cook the bacon while the wrap bakes. Let the wrap cool slightly, then peel off the paper.
5. Add the bacon, Parmesan, spinach and tomatoes, then fold to enclose the filling.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
