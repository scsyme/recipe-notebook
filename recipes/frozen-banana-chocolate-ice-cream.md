# Frozen banana chocolate ice cream

Serves 1.

## Ingredients

- 1 large frozen banana (about 150 g), in chunks
- About 30 ml unsweetened almond milk or milk of choice
- 1 tsp cocoa powder (about 3 g)
- 1 tsp peanut butter (about 5 g)

## Method

1. Blend the frozen banana chunks, milk, cocoa and peanut butter until smooth and spoonable.
2. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for 30 minutes before serving.
