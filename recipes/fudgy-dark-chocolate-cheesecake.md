# Fudgy dark chocolate cheesecake

Makes one 15 cm cheesecake.

## Ingredients

- About 220 g cottage cheese
- About 200 g Greek yoghurt
- 2 large eggs
- About 50 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 120 ml maple syrup or honey
- About 50 g oat flour or almond flour
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 165°C and line a 15 cm baking tin.
2. Blend all the ingredients until smooth, then pour into the tin.
3. Bake for 45–50 minutes. Look for set edges and a centre that still wobbles slightly.
4. Refrigerate for at least 8 hours, preferably overnight, before slicing.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
