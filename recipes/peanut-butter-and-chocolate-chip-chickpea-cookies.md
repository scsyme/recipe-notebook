# Peanut butter and chocolate chip chickpea cookies

Makes 12 cookies.

## Ingredients

- 600 g cooked chickpeas, drained weight
- 40 g smooth peanut butter
- 20 g honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 30 g chocolate chips (the source uses Noshu)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
2. Blend the chickpeas, peanut butter, honey, vanilla and baking powder until smooth. Fold in the chocolate chips.
3. Shape into 12 cookies, arrange on a baking tray and flatten slightly.
4. Bake for 15–20 minutes until lightly golden. Let the cookies cool so they firm up.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
