# Single-serving yoghurt cheesecake

Makes 1 generous serving.

## Ingredients

- About 225 g plain high-protein or 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 large egg
- 2 tbsp honey
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- A tiny pinch of salt
- A little fat for greasing
- Berries, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 165°C and lightly grease a ramekin or small baking dish.
2. Mix the yoghurt, egg, honey, vanilla and salt until smooth, then pour into the dish.
3. Bake for about 20 minutes until the edges set and the centre still wobbles.
4. Cool, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight. Top with berries to serve.

The source does not specify a fan or conventional oven.
